About Precious

Building a Life of Purpose, Possibility & Impact

Precious Mceshiet Diri is a purpose-driven entrepreneur, business leader, real estate professional, speaker and advocate for women's empowerment.

For her, success is not defined solely by what she builds, but by the lives she touches and the people she inspires along the way.

What She Stands For

Purpose

Living intentionally and pursuing work that creates meaningful impact.

Entrepreneurship

Transforming ideas and opportunities into sustainable ventures that create value.

Empowerment

Encouraging people, especially women, to discover their potential and take action.

Excellence

Striving for quality, growth and continuous improvement in every endeavour.

Impact

Creating opportunities and building a legacy that positively influences others.

Integrity

Leading with honesty, consistency and strong principles in every relationship.

Her Journey

From Vision to Impact

Precious believes that every meaningful journey begins with the courage to start.

Over the years, she has transformed ideas into ventures and opportunities into platforms for growth — across real estate, fashion, and events and hospitality — each one a different expression of her creativity and commitment to excellence.

Beyond Business

Empowering Women to Discover Their Potential

Beyond entrepreneurship, Precious is deeply passionate about helping women recognise their gifts, embrace their potential and pursue lives of purpose.

Through her women's empowerment initiative, She Activates Purpose, she creates conversations and opportunities that encourage women to look beyond limitations and take meaningful steps towards becoming who they were created to be.

She believes every woman has something valuable within her — an idea, a gift, a vision — that can be developed, shared and used to create impact.
